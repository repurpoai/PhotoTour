Core Foundation

The Exposure Triangle

Exposure is the delicate optical balance of three interconnected physical pillars. Changing one parameter requires balancing another to maintain optimal light while shaping artistic intent.

Aperture (f-stop) → Depth of Field Controls the physical diameter of the lens diaphragm. Wide apertures (f/1.4) create creamy background bokeh; narrow apertures (f/16) render foreground to background in crisp focus.
Shutter Speed → Motion & Time Controls how long the physical shutter curtains expose the sensor. Fast speeds (1/2000s) freeze hummingbirds; slow speeds (2s) paint silky water and neon light trails.
ISO → Digital Gain & Noise Grain Amplifies the electrical signal from sensor pixels. Low ISO (100) produces ultra-clean fidelity; high ISO (6400+) captures dim environments at the expense of grain.

Optical Physics Explained

What is Lens Compression?

Focal length does not merely "magnify" the picture; it fundamentally changes the apparent spatial relationship between foreground subjects and background scenery.

  • Wide Angle (14mm - 24mm): Stretches distance, making background mountains or cityscapes appear tiny and leagues away.
  • Standard (50mm): Closely replicates normal human eye perception and true-to-life proportions without distortion.
  • Telephoto (85mm - 200mm): Pulls distant background elements forward, making far-off hills look like they are right behind your portrait subject!

Field Reference Cheat Sheets

Printable quick-reference visual cards for exposure settings, focal length selection, and shooting scenarios.

1. The Exposure Triangle

Pillar More Light ← → Less Light Creative Side-Effect
Aperture f/1.4, f/2.0 (Wide) f/16, f/22 (Narrow) Shallow DoF ↔ Deep Sharpness
Shutter 1/2s, 2s (Slow) 1/1000s, 1/4000s (Fast) Motion Blur ↔ Frozen Action
ISO 6400, 12800 (High) 100, 200 (Low) Digital Noise ↔ Clean Signal

2. Shutter Speed Quick Guide

1/4000 - 1/2000s Hummingbirds, water droplet impacts, racing cars, supersonic action.
1/1000 - 1/500s Sports athletes running, birds in flight, playful dogs, moving cyclists.
1/250 - 1/125s Standard everyday handheld portraiture, street walking, casual candids.
1/60s Handheld threshold limit! Slower speeds require tripod or optical stabilization.
1/4s - 2s Silky cascading waterfalls, ocean tide surf blur, intentional panning streaks.
5s - 30s Highway night car light trails, glassy city reflections, Milky Way astrophotography.

3. Aperture (f-stop) Field Scale

f/1.4 - f/2.0 Dreamy buttery bokeh portraits, extreme subject isolation, dim indoor light.
f/2.8 - f/4.0 Environmental portraits, street scenes, group portraits with sharp eyes.
f/8.0 - f/11 The lens "Sweet Spot" for maximum edge-to-edge optical sharpness. Landscapes.
f/16 - f/22 Maximum depth of field, sunburst star effects on point light sources. (Beware diffraction).

4. White Balance & Kelvin Scale

2000K - 2500K Candlelight, open fireplace, sunrise red ember glow.
3000K - 3200K Tungsten household lamps, cozy incandescent interior lighting.
5200K - 5500K Standard direct midday sunlight, studio electronic strobes. Neutral baseline.
6500K Overcast cloudy sky (adds warmth to counteract cooler gray sky).
7500K - 8500K Deep open shadow on a clear day, dusk blue hour.

5. Focal Length Selection Matrix

14mm - 24mm Ultra-wide. Sweeping alpine vistas, architecture interiors, dynamic astrophotography.
35mm - 50mm Standard / Documentary. "The Human Perspective". Street photography, photojournalism.
85mm - 135mm Short Telephoto. The gold standard for flattering headshots with zero facial distortion.
200mm - 400mm+ Super Telephoto. Shy wildlife, distant sports athletes, extreme landscape compression.

6. Field Shooting Recipes

Golden Hour Portrait f/1.8 - f/2.8 • 1/250s • ISO 100 • WB: Shade or 6500K
Sharp Mountain Vista f/8.0 - f/11 • 1/125s • ISO 100 • Tripod • Focus at 1/3 depth
Silky Waterfall Flow f/11 - f/16 • 1/2s - 2s • ISO 100 • ND Filter • Tripod
Fast Action Sports f/2.8 - f/4.0 • 1/1000s+ • Auto ISO (100-3200) • Continuous AF
Milky Way Astro f/1.4 - f/2.8 • 15s - 20s (500 Rule) • ISO 3200 - 6400 • Manual Focus ∞
