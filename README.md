# 📸 PhotoTour — Learn Photography by Experimenting

**Understand the camera. Master the frame. Create with confidence.**

PhotoTour is an interactive photography learning platform designed to help beginners and aspiring photographers master the art and science of photography through hands-on experimentation. Instead of relying on lengthy tutorials and theory-heavy explanations, PhotoTour provides a visual learning environment where users can explore camera settings, experiment with composition, and understand how photographic decisions affect the final image.

Whether you're using a smartphone, DSLR, or mirrorless camera, PhotoTour helps you build practical photography skills in a virtual environment — without expensive equipment or the fear of making mistakes.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📷 1. Virtual Camera Simulator

Experiment with camera settings and instantly understand their effects on a photograph.

* **ISO:** Explore brightness, image noise, and sensor sensitivity.
* **Shutter Speed:** Learn to freeze motion or create motion blur.
* **Aperture:** Understand exposure and depth of field.
* **White Balance:** Experiment with color temperature and color casts.
* **Focus:** Explore focus points, sharpness, and background blur.
* **Exposure:** Understand how camera settings work together to control brightness.
* **Focal Length:** Compare wide-angle, standard, and telephoto perspectives.

Explore realistic photographic scenarios and see how changing individual settings influences the overall image.

### 🌄 2. Multiple Photography Scenarios

Practice photography across a variety of real-world-inspired scenes:

* 🧑 Portrait photography
* 🏞️ Landscape photography
* 🌅 Sunset and golden-hour photography
* 🌃 Night and low-light photography
* 🦅 Wildlife photography
* 🏃 Sports and moving subjects
* 🌸 Macro and close-up photography

Each scene provides opportunities to experiment with camera settings and understand how to adapt to different lighting conditions and subjects.

### 🎯 3. Interactive 3D Composition Lab

Explore the art of composition in an interactive three-dimensional environment.

* Navigate virtual 3D scenes.
* Reposition cameras and adjust viewing angles.
* Experiment with focal lengths and perspective.
* Compare different camera positions and framing choices.
* Visualize composition guides directly within the scene.

**Learn essential composition techniques:**

* Rule of Thirds
* Leading Lines
* Symmetry and Balance
* Negative Space
* Natural Framing
* Foreground, Midground, and Background Layering
* Golden Ratio

Understand not only how to compose a photograph, but also why one composition may communicate a stronger visual story than another.

### 🎓 4. Guided Learning Paths

Follow structured lessons that take you from photography fundamentals to more advanced concepts.

* Camera fundamentals and exposure
* Aperture, shutter speed, and ISO
* Understanding lenses and focal lengths
* Lighting and white balance
* Focus and autofocus modes
* Sensor sizes and crop factor
* RAW vs. JPEG
* Dynamic range and image quality
* Photography genres and creative techniques

Learn at your own pace with interactive demonstrations and practical examples.

### 🏆 5. Interactive Photography Challenges

Put your knowledge into practice with guided challenges designed to develop real photographic skills.

Example challenges include:

* **Freeze the Action:** Capture a moving subject using an appropriate shutter speed.
* **Create Background Blur:** Experiment with aperture, focal length, and subject distance.
* **Capture a Sharp Landscape:** Explore depth of field and suitable exposure settings.
* **Master Low Light:** Balance ISO, shutter speed, and aperture.
* **Find the Perfect Frame:** Improve composition using visual guides.
* **Capture Golden Hour:** Experiment with warm light and white balance.

Each challenge encourages experimentation and provides feedback to help users understand their photographic decisions.

### 📊 6. Personalized Learning Dashboard

Track your photography learning journey in one place.

* Monitor learning progress.
* Track completed lessons and challenges.
* Save favorite compositions.
* Revisit previous experiments.
* Identify areas for improvement.
* Build confidence through consistent practice.

### 🎨 7. Visual-First, Responsive Experience

PhotoTour emphasizes intuitive controls, interactive visuals, and practical learning.

* Beginner-friendly interface
* Interactive camera controls
* Visual comparisons of photographic effects
* Responsive layouts for different screen sizes
* Hands-on experimentation without physical camera equipment

The goal is to make photography concepts easier to understand through observation, exploration, and practice.

## 🚀 How PhotoTour Works

1. **Choose a scene** — Select a portrait, landscape, night scene, or another photography scenario.
2. **Adjust camera settings** — Experiment with ISO, aperture, shutter speed, focus, and focal length.
3. **Observe the results** — Explore how each adjustment changes brightness, motion, depth of field, noise, and color.
4. **Experiment with composition** — Move the virtual camera and apply composition guides in the 3D lab.
5. **Complete challenges** — Apply what you've learned to practical photography exercises.
6. **Track your progress** — Revisit lessons, save experiments, and continue improving.
